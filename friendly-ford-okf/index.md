# Friendly Ford

An Open Knowledge Format (OKF) v0.1 bundle describing Friendly Ford, a Ford dealership at 2800 N Telegraph Rd, Monroe, MI 48162 serving Monroe County and the greater Detroit-Toledo area. It covers the dealership profile, new and pre-owned vehicle sales, the "Service Your Way" service model, collision center, mobile service, financing, staff, reviews, and AI-optimized discovery endpoints.
