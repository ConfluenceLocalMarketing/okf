# Friendly Ford FAQ

Common questions and answers about Friendly Ford's sales, service, and dealership programs. Synthesized from the dealership's website content (synthesized).

## Buying

**What models does Friendly Ford sell?**
Friendly Ford sells the full Ford lineup including the F-150, Super Duty, Ranger, Maverick, Bronco, Explorer, Escape, Expedition, Mustang, and Mustang Mach-E, plus commercial Transit vans.

**Does Friendly Ford offer a trade-in appraisal?**
Yes. Friendly Ford offers a fast trade-in value tool powered by Kelley Blue Book Instant Cash Offer.

## Financing

**Does Friendly Ford offer financing?**
Yes. The finance center offers no-hassle financing and lease options for new and pre-owned vehicles, including Ford program offers with APR and cash incentives.

## Service

**Does Friendly Ford offer mobile service?**
Yes. Under "Service Your Way," customers can choose in-person service, mobile service at home or office, or pickup and delivery.

**What amenities does the service center offer?**
Free coffee and Wi-Fi, loaner vehicles, a local shuttle service, and a free car wash with service.

## Company

**Is Friendly Ford part of a larger group?**
Friendly Ford shares the Monroe campus with Friendly Lincoln under the combined "Friendly Ford Lincoln" brand.
