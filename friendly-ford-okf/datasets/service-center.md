# Friendly Ford Service Center

Friendly Ford operates a full service center at 2800 N Telegraph Rd, Monroe, MI, under a "Service Your Way" model offering three service options.

## Service Your Way

- **In-Person Service** - Traditional service appointments
- **Mobile Service** - Technicians dispatched to the customer's home or office
- **Pick-Up & Delivery** - Vehicle pickup and delivery

## Service Amenities

- Free coffee and Wi-Fi
- Loaner vehicles
- Local shuttle service
- Free car wash with service
- Factory-trained technicians and OEM parts

## Collision Center

A dedicated collision center provides body shop services at (734) 243-6900, staffed by a Collision Center Manager, technicians, a painter, and an estimator.
