# Friendly Ford

Friendly Ford is a Ford dealership located at 2800 N Telegraph Rd, Monroe, MI 48162. It shares the Friendly Auto Group campus with Friendly Lincoln, operating under the combined "Friendly Ford Lincoln" brand. The dealership serves Monroe County and the greater Detroit-Toledo area.

## Service Area

Monroe County and nearby Wayne County communities including Monroe, Riverview, Trenton, and Woodhaven, Michigan.

## About

Friendly Ford's mission is to be "the automotive home of drivers in the Monroe MI area." The dealership's tagline is "Friendly - It's not just a name, it's the way we do business!" It is led by President Tom Shankie and Dealer Principal & General Manager Nicole Ernst.

## Departments and Features

- **Sales** - New and pre-owned Ford cars, trucks, and SUVs
- **Service Center** - "Service Your Way" with in-person service, mobile service, and pickup & delivery
- **Collision Center** - Full body shop at (734) 243-6900
- **Parts Center** - Genuine Ford parts
- **Finance Center** - No-hassle financing and leasing
- **EV & Hybrid** - Electric and hybrid vehicle inventory

## Service Amenities

Free coffee and Wi-Fi, loaner vehicles, local shuttle service, and a free car wash with service.
