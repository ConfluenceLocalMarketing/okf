# Ford Model Comparison

The Ford lineup sold at Friendly Ford spans compact crossovers to heavy-duty commercial trucks, with electric options.

| Model | Segment | Body Style | Powertrain Options |
|---|---|---|---|
| Maverick | Compact pickup | Truck | Gasoline, Hybrid |
| Ranger | Mid-size pickup | Truck | Gasoline |
| F-150 | Full-size pickup | Truck | Gasoline, Hybrid, Electric (Lightning) |
| F-150 Lightning | Full-size electric pickup | Truck | Electric |
| Super Duty (F-250/F-350/F-450/F-550/F-600) | Heavy-duty pickup | Truck | Gasoline, Diesel |
| Transit (150/250/350) | Commercial van | Van | Gasoline |
| Bronco Sport | Compact off-road SUV | SUV | Gasoline |
| Bronco | Mid-size off-road SUV | SUV | Gasoline |
| Escape | Compact SUV | SUV | Gasoline, Hybrid |
| Explorer | Mid-size three-row SUV | SUV | Gasoline, Hybrid |
| Expedition / Expedition Max | Full-size SUV | SUV | Gasoline |
| Mustang Mach-E | Electric crossover | SUV | Electric |
| Mustang | Sports car | Convertible/Coupe | Gasoline |

## Notes

- The F-150 Lightning and Mustang Mach-E represent Ford's electric lineup.
- Super Duty and Transit models support Friendly Ford's commercial and work truck focus.
- The Mustang is the lone convertible/coupe in the lineup.
