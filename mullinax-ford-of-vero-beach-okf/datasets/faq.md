# Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the dealership, Up Front Pricing, custom factory orders and the buying process at Mullinax Ford of Vero Beach.

## Pricing FAQ

### Is there a dealer fee?

No. Mullinax Ford of Vero Beach charges a $0 dealer fee. The advertised price is the price you pay, plus tax and title.

### Does the dealership negotiate?

No. The dealership uses Up Front Pricing, a no-haggle model where the sticker price is the final price.

### What is included in the Up Front Price?

The Up Front Price includes Mullinax Discounts, Factory Rebates and Ford Credit Financing Rebates; other rebates such as College, Military or Private offers may apply in addition.

## Buying FAQ

### Can I custom order a new Ford?

Yes. If the desired configuration is not in stock, the dealership can place a custom factory order. Ford does not charge extra for customer-ordered vehicles and incentives can be applied at order placement or delivery.

### Can I buy a vehicle online?

Yes. The dealership supports online vehicle shopping with digital tools for pre-approval, payment calculation and trade-in valuation, and vehicles can be delivered within the Mullinax network.

### What areas does the dealership serve?

The dealership serves Vero Beach, Fort Pierce, Sebastian, Fellsmere and the surrounding Treasure Coast, financing customers from Port St. Lucie to Palm Bay.

### Can I sell my used car without buying a new one?

Yes. The dealership buys used cars for cash at [sell-your-used-car-vero-beach-fl](https://www.mullinaxfordverobeach.com/sell-your-used-car-vero-beach-fl/).

## Service FAQ

### What does the dealership service?

The service center handles all Ford makes and models, and Quick Lane services all vehicle makes and models. The body shop handles any make or model.

### Does Quick Lane require an appointment?

No. Quick Lane provides service without an appointment, with service while you wait.
