# Financing

Mullinax Ford of Vero Beach offers vehicle financing and leasing through Ford Credit and partner lenders, with online tools for pre-approval, payments and trade-in valuation. The finance department serves customers from Vero Beach, Fort Pierce, Sebastian, Port St. Lucie and Palm Bay.

## Financing Tools

- Apply for financing online at [apply-for-financing](https://www.mullinaxfordverobeach.com/finance/apply-for-financing/)
- Payment calculator at [payment-calculator](https://www.mullinaxfordverobeach.com/payment-calculator/)
- Value your trade at [value-your-trade](https://www.mullinaxfordverobeach.com/value-your-trade/)
- Buying power estimator at [whats-my-buying-power](https://www.mullinaxfordverobeach.com/whats-my-buying-power/)
- Expedite paperwork online at [expedite-your-paperwork](https://www.mullinaxfordverobeach.com/expedite-your-paperwork/)

## Credit Programs

- **Best credit** - "I never miss a payment!": helps find the best interest rate for the next vehicle purchase
- **OK credit** - "I've missed a payment or two.": special arrangements with local and national lenders
- **Not so good credit** - "I need to rebuild my credit...": programs to get into a car and rebuild credit

## Services

- New and used vehicle financing
- Leasing through Ford Credit
- Pre-approval before visiting the dealership
- Financing for all credit situations via partner lenders
- Ford A, Z, D and X plans available to eligible buyers
- Ford finance specials at [new-vehicle-specials](https://www.mullinaxfordverobeach.com/new-vehicles/new-vehicle-specials/)
