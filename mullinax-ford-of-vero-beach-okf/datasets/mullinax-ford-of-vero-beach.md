# Mullinax Ford of Vero Beach

## Overview

Mullinax Ford of Vero Beach is a full-line Ford dealership located at 488 U.S. 1, Vero Beach, FL 32962, serving Vero Beach, Fort Pierce, Sebastian, Fellsmere and the wider Treasure Coast of Florida. It is part of the Mullinax Automotive family of no-haggle Ford stores and markets Up Front Pricing with zero dealer fees.
