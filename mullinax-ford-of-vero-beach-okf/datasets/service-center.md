# Service Center and Quick Lane

Mullinax Ford of Vero Beach operates a full service center staffed by factory-trained technicians using genuine Ford parts, a Quick Lane Tire and Auto Center for fast maintenance on all makes and models, and a body shop and collision center.

## Service Center

- Factory-trained Ford technicians
- Genuine Ford and Motorcraft parts
- Online appointment scheduling at [schedule-service](https://www.mullinaxfordverobeach.com/service/contact-service/)
- Service coupons and specials at [service-specials](https://www.mullinaxfordverobeach.com/service/service-specials/)
- Service for Ford owners includes oil changes, batteries, tires, brakes and air conditioning
- Serves the Vero Beach, Fort Pierce and Sebastian area

## Quick Lane Tire and Auto Center

Quick Lane at Mullinax Ford of Vero Beach provides maintenance and repair services for all vehicle makes and models, with no appointment needed and service while you wait.

### Quick Lane Services

- Synthetic and synthetic blend oil changes with oil filter replacement
- Battery testing, charging and new battery installation
- Tire rotation and balancing, flat tire repairs and new tire installation
- Brake pad replacement, rotor and caliper repair, brake line repair
- Air filter replacement
- Windshield wiper replacement
- Belt and hose replacement
- Wheel alignment
- Spark plug replacement
- Electrical repair
- Cooling system checks (hose damage and leaks, operating temperatures)
- Belts and hoses inspections (wear, cracks, tension, leaks, pinch-test)

### Quick Lane Highlights

- No appointment necessary; service while you wait
- The Works Fuel Saver Package (oil change plus tire rotation, brake inspection, battery test and Vehicle Check-Up Report)
- 16 name-brand tire lines including Goodyear, Michelin, Continental, Bridgestone, BFGoodrich, Pirelli, Firestone, Hankook, Yokohama, Toyo, Nitto, Falken, Dunlop, Kelly, Uniroyal and General Tire
- Low Price Tire Guarantee
- Genuine Motorcraft parts
- Services all makes and models
- Quality parts from trusted brands like Motorcraft, Goodyear, Continental Tire and Michelin

### Service Facts

- Oil changes generally recommended every 5,000 to 10,000 miles depending on vehicle
- Tires can last 30,000 to 60,000 miles; replace when tread wears to 3/32 inch (2.4 mm) or less
- Brakes can last 30,000 to 60,000 miles; brake pad replacement roughly $190 per axle
- Typical car battery capacity is 12.6 volts with 300 to 760 CCA; in Florida batteries typically last two to three years

## Body Shop and Collision Center

The body shop at [body-shop-collision-center](https://www.mullinaxfordverobeach.com/body-shop-collision-center/) provides cosmetic and structural repairs for any make or model, serving the Vero Beach, Fort Pierce and Treasure Coast area.

### Body Shop Services

- Scratch and dent repair, including paintless dent repair and paint touch-up
- Painting and paint repair with exact color matching
- Rust repair
- Frame repair back to factory specifications
- Collision damage and repair returning vehicles to manufacturer specifications

### Body Shop Scheduling

- Open six days a week; schedule online with year, make, model and mileage
- Preferred appointment time slots: Anytime, Morning, Mid-day, Afternoon, Evening
