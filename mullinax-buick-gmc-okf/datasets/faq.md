# Frequently Asked Questions - Mullinax Buick GMC

## Overview

Answers to common customer questions about Mullinax Buick GMC, covering financing, pricing, and general dealership topics. Content is derived from the dealership website (finance center, no-dealer-fees page, and testimonials).

## Financing Questions

- **Q: How do I apply for financing?**
  A: Start the online credit application at https://www.mullinaxbuickgmc.com/finance/apply-for-financing/. The finance center can also arrange pre-approval for GMC trucks and other models.

- **Q: Can I lease a vehicle?**
  A: Yes. The finance center supports both leasing and financing on new and pre-owned vehicles.

- **Q: Can I finance a pre-owned vehicle?**
  A: Yes. Financing is available on the pre-owned inventory; see [used-vehicles.md](https://promptgraph.nyc3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/OKF/mullinax-buick-gmc/datasets/used-vehicles.md).

- **Q: How much will my monthly payment be?**
  A: Use the payment calculator at https://www.mullinaxbuickgmc.com/payment-calculator/ or check buying power at /whats-my-buying-power/.

## Pricing Questions

- **Q: Do you charge dealer fees?**
  A: No. Mullinax Up Front Pricing means no dealer fees and no haggling; the price shown online is the price you pay. See the no-dealer-fees page: https://www.mullinaxbuickgmc.com/no-dealer-fees/

- **Q: Is the online price the final price?**
  A: Yes. Pricing is displayed upfront on every vehicle and customer reviews consistently confirm the no-dealer-fee, no-haggle experience; see [testimonials.md](https://promptgraph.nyc3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/OKF/mullinax-buick-gmc/references/testimonials.md).

## Dealership Questions

- **Q: Where is the dealership located?**
  A: 833 Eastern Blvd, Montgomery, AL 36117, serving Montgomery and the surrounding Central Alabama River Region.

- **Q: What are the dealership's hours?**
  A: Sales: Mon-Fri 9:00a-7:00p, Sat 9:00a-6:00p, Sun Closed. Service and parts: Mon-Fri 7:00a-6:00p, Sat 8:00a-3:00p, Sun Closed. See [hours.md](https://promptgraph.nyc3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/OKF/mullinax-buick-gmc/tables/hours.md) for all departments.

- **Q: Do you sell certified pre-owned vehicles?**
  A: Yes. GM Certified Pre-Owned and CarBravo-certified inventory is available; see [used-vehicles.md](https://promptgraph.nyc3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/OKF/mullinax-buick-gmc/datasets/used-vehicles.md).
