# Pre-Owned Vehicles - Mullinax Buick GMC

## Overview

Mullinax Buick GMC carries a pre-owned inventory spanning Buick, GMC, and all makes and models. Used vehicles are priced with the same Up Front Pricing policy as new vehicles: transparent pricing, no dealer fees, and no haggling.

## Used Inventory

- **Used Buick** - Enclave, Encore GX, Envision, Envista, and other makes.
- **Used GMC** - Sierra, Terrain, Acadia, Yukon, Canyon, and other makes.
- **All makes and models** - Chevrolet, Ford, Toyota, Honda, Nissan, Hyundai, Kia, Jeep, and more.
- **GM Certified Pre-Owned** - certified units flagged in the inventory with warranty coverage.
- **CarBravo** - GM's certified used-vehicle program with online buying and delivery; see https://buy.mullinaxbuickgmc.com/carbravo/.
