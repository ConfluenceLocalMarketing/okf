# Financing & Auto Loans - Mullinax Buick GMC

## Overview

The finance center at Mullinax Buick GMC arranges new and used vehicle financing and leasing. Customers can begin the credit application online and complete the process in-store or through the finance team.

## Finance Center

- **Page**: https://www.mullinaxbuickgmc.com/finance/
- Lease or finance on new and pre-owned vehicles
- Online finance application: https://www.mullinaxbuickgmc.com/finance/apply-for-financing/
- Payment calculator: https://www.mullinaxbuickgmc.com/payment-calculator/
- Pre-qualify: https://www.mullinaxbuickgmc.com/pre-qualify/ and /get-prequalified/

## Financing Programs

- **New vehicle financing** - financing on any new Buick or GMC vehicle.
- **Used vehicle financing** - competitive rates on pre-owned inventory.
- **Leasing** - GM lease programs (referenced in promotions such as GMC Sierra 1500 lease deals).
- **Pre-approval** - online credit application and pre-approval for GMC trucks; see [prompts.md](https://promptgraph.nyc3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/OKF/mullinax-buick-gmc/references/prompts.md) prompt #3 (Auto Loan Pre-Approval).
- **Flexible financing** - flexible loan options and financing specials (see prompts #10, Flexible Auto Financing).

## Trade-In

- **Value your trade**: online trade appraisal at https://www.mullinaxbuickgmc.com/value-your-trade/
- **Trade-in valuation** applied toward any purchase; see [used-vehicles.md](https://promptgraph.nyc3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/OKF/mullinax-buick-gmc/datasets/used-vehicles.md).
- **What's My Buying Power**: https://www.mullinaxbuickgmc.com/whats-my-buying-power/
