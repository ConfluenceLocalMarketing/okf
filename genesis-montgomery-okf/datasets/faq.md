# FAQ - Genesis Montgomery

## Overview

Frequently asked questions and answers for Genesis Montgomery, synthesized from the dealership's published AI prompt set and site pages. There is no standalone FAQ page on the website; these answers reflect the dealership's own published Q&A content.

## Buying and Inventory

**How do I find available inventory?**

Browse new inventory at https://www.genesismontgomery.com/cars/new-inventory and used inventory at https://www.genesismontgomery.com/cars/certified-pre-owned. The site supports model filters, drivetrain filters, and search.

**Does the dealership sell all-wheel-drive Genesis models?**

Yes. The new inventory is primarily AWD (86 of 107 units as of 2026-08-07), including the GV80, GV70, and G70. AWD models are a focus of the lineup in the Montgomery market.

**Can I test drive a luxury SUV?**

Yes. The dealership invites test drives of the GV70, GV80, GV80 Coupe, and other models. Contact sales at (334) 373-1781 to schedule.

## Financing

**Can I get pre-approved before visiting?**

Yes. Complete the online credit application at https://www.genesismontgomery.com/apply-for-financing or the financing page to get pre-approved before visiting.

**What financing options are available?**

Lease and loan options up to 72 months are available, with competitive rates from top lenders and credit unions. A payment calculator is available at https://www.genesismontgomery.com/payment-calculator.

**Are there special finance programs?**

Yes, including College Grad, Military, First Responder, Loyalty, and Competitive Owner programs. See [financing.md](https://promptgraph.nyc3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/OKF/genesis-montgomery/datasets/financing.md) for current offers.

## Trade-In

**Can I trade in my current vehicle?**

Yes. Get a trade-in valuation at https://www.genesismontgomery.com/value-your-trade.

## Service

**Do I need an appointment for service?**

Appointments are recommended and can be scheduled online from the service page: https://www.genesismontgomery.com/service.

**Is a car wash included with service?**

Yes. Every service visit includes a complimentary car wash, and complimentary valet service is available.

**Are the technicians factory trained?**

Yes. Service is performed by factory-trained, Genesis-certified technicians using genuine OEM parts.

## Business Hours

**What are the dealership's hours?**

Sales and the main showroom are open Mon-Fri 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM and Sat 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, closed Sunday. Service is open Mon-Fri 7:30 AM to 6:00 PM. See [hours.md](https://promptgraph.nyc3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/OKF/genesis-montgomery/tables/hours.md) for details.
