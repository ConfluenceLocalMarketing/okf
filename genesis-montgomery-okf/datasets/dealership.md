# Genesis Montgomery

## Overview

Genesis Montgomery is an authorized Genesis dealership located at 4695 Carmichael Road, Montgomery, AL 36106. The dealership sells new Genesis vehicles and pre-owned vehicles of all makes, and operates a full-service center with factory-trained technicians. Marketing tagline used on site materials: "Luxury Meets Innovation" and "Steeped in Luxury, Driven By Innovation."

The dealership advertises up-front pricing with no dealer markup on new vehicles.
