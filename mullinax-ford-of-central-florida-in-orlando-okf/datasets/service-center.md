# Service Center & Quick Lane - Mullinax Ford of Central Florida

## Overview

The Mullinax Ford of Central Florida service department performs factory-trained maintenance and repair using Ford genuine parts. A Quick Lane provides express service for all makes and models.

## Service Department

- **Hours**: Mon-Fri 7:30a-6:00p, Sat 8:00a-4:00p, Sun Closed
- **Phone**: (888) 207-2696
- **Address**: 1551 E Semoran Blvd, Apopka, FL 32703
- Ford-trained and ASE-certified technicians
- Ford genuine / OEM parts and accessories
- Complimentary multi-point inspection with service

## Services Offered

- Oil changes and filter service
- Brake service and repair
- Tire rotation, alignment, and replacement
- Battery testing and replacement
- Air conditioning service and repair
- Engine and transmission diagnostics and repair
- Scheduled maintenance per the Owner's Scheduled Maintenance Guide
- Ford factory-scheduled maintenance services

## Convenience Options

- **Mobile Service**: Service a vehicle at a customer's home or workplace.
- **Pickup & Delivery**: Vehicle picked up and returned for service.
- **Online scheduling**: https://www.mullinaxfordfl.com/schedule-service-appointment
- **Service specials and coupons**: https://www.mullinaxfordfl.com/service-coupons

## Quick Lane

- **Phone**: (855) 340-9009
