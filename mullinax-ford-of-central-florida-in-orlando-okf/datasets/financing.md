# Financing & Auto Loans - Mullinax Ford of Central Florida

## Overview

The finance department at Mullinax Ford of Central Florida arranges new and used vehicle financing and leasing. Customers can begin the credit application online and work with finance staff in-store.

## Online Credit Application

- Apply online at https://www.mullinaxfordfl.com/apply-online
- Fast pre-qualification with no impact on credit score for initial quote.
- Applications are submitted to multiple lending partners for competitive rates.

## Financing Programs

- **New vehicle financing** - manufacturer rates and programs on new Ford vehicles.
- **Used vehicle financing** - competitive rates on pre-owned inventory.
- **Bad credit financing** - programs for buyers with less-than-perfect credit.
- **No money down options** - depending on qualification.
- **Leasing** - Ford lease programs with flexible mileage terms.

## Lending Partners

- Work with a network of national and local lenders ("dozens of lenders").
- Financing available for buyers across all credit backgrounds.
- Dedicated finance team to match buyers with an appropriate lender.

## Tools

- **Payment calculator**: estimate monthly payments by price, down payment, and term.
- **Trade-in valuation**: online appraisal applied toward any purchase; see [used-vehicles.md](used-vehicles.md).
- **Value your trade**: combine trade value with financing to reduce the financed amount.
