# Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the dealership, Up Front Pricing, custom factory orders and the buying process at Mullinax Ford of West Palm Beach.

## Pricing FAQ

### Is there a dealer fee?

No. Mullinax Ford of West Palm Beach charges a $0 dealer fee and no electronic filing fees. The advertised price is the price you pay, plus tax and title.

### Does the dealership negotiate?

No. The dealership uses Up Front Pricing, a no-haggle model where the sticker price is the final price.

### What is included in the Up Front Price?

The Up Front Price includes Mullinax Discounts, Factory Rebates and Ford Credit Financing Rebates; other rebates such as College, Military or Private offers may apply in addition.

## Buying FAQ

### Can I custom order a new Ford?

Yes. If the desired configuration is not in stock, the dealership can place a custom factory order.

### Can I buy a vehicle online?

Yes. The dealership supports online vehicle shopping with tools for financing pre-approval and trade-in valuation, and vehicles can be delivered within the Mullinax network.

### What areas does the dealership serve?

The dealership serves West Palm Beach, Lake Park, Palm Beach Gardens, Riviera Beach, Lake Worth, Delray Beach and Boynton Beach in Palm Beach County.

### Can I sell my used car without buying a new one?

Yes. The dealership buys used cars for cash at [sell-your-used-car-palm-beach-fl](https://www.mullinaxfordwestpalm.com/sell-your-used-car-palm-beach-fl).

## Service FAQ

### What does the dealership service?

The Service Center handles Ford, Lincoln and Mercury vehicles with Ford-trained technicians. Quick Lane services all vehicle makes and models with walk-in maintenance.

### Does Quick Lane require an appointment?

No. Quick Lane provides walk-in service with no appointment necessary, and there is a mobile service option that comes to the customer's location.
