# Financing

Mullinax Ford of West Palm Beach offers vehicle financing and leasing through Ford Credit and partner lenders, with online tools for credit application, pre-approval and trade-in valuation. The finance department serves customers across Palm Beach County.

## Financing Tools

- Apply for financing online at [apply-online](https://www.mullinaxfordwestpalm.com/apply-online)
- Financing details at [financing-auto-loans](https://www.mullinaxfordwestpalm.com/financing-auto-loans)
- Sell your used car for cash at [sell-your-used-car-palm-beach-fl](https://www.mullinaxfordwestpalm.com/sell-your-used-car-palm-beach-fl)
- Work trucks and commercial fleet financing available through the Commercial department

## Services

- New and used vehicle financing
- Leasing through Ford Credit
- Pre-approval before visiting the dealership
- Flexible auto loan options and financing specials
- Financing for all credit situations via partner lenders
