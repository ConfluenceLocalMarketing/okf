# Service Center and Quick Lane

Mullinax Ford of West Palm Beach offers two repair options: a Quick Lane auto center for walk-in maintenance on all vehicle makes and models, and a Service Center staffed by Ford-trained technicians for Ford, Lincoln and Mercury vehicles. A mobile service program brings technicians to the customer's location.

## Option 1: Quick Lane Maintenance

The Quick Lane at [quick-lane](https://www.mullinaxfordwestpalm.com/quick-lane) offers basic maintenance and repair services on all vehicle makes and models, with walk-in service and no appointment necessary.

### Quick Lane Services

- Conventional and synthetic oil changes with oil filter replacement
- Tire rotation and balancing, flat tire repairs and new tire installation
- Battery testing, charging and new battery installation
- Brake pad replacement, rotor and caliper repair, brake line repair
- Air filter replacement
- Windshield wiper replacement
- Belt and hose replacement
- Wheel alignment
- Spark plug replacement
- Basic electrical repair
- Fluid check and refill
- Recall servicing and parts installation
- Lamp repair and bulb replacement
- Software updates
- Multi-point inspections

### Quick Lane Highlights

- Walk-in service, no appointment necessary
- 12 service bays total, including four bays dedicated to Ford commercial service that handle big trucks and work vans
- Low tire price guarantee
- Genuine Motorcraft parts
- Lifetime warranty on brakes
- Comfortable customer lounge with refreshments and free WiFi
- Services all makes and models
- Serves West Palm Beach, the greater Palm Beach area, Delray Beach and Boynton Beach

### Service Facts

- Oil changes generally recommended every 5,000 to 10,000 miles depending on the vehicle; four-cylinder cars usually take four quarts while high-performance V8s may need as much as 12 quarts
- Tires can last 30,000 to 60,000 miles; replace when tread wears to 3/32 inch (2.4 mm) or less
- Brakes can last 30,000 to 60,000 miles; brake pad replacement roughly $190 per axle, new brakes about $95 per wheel including parts and installation
- Typical car battery capacity is 12.6 volts with 300 to 760 CCA; in Florida batteries typically last two to three years
- Battery charging can be done in 30 minutes to an hour with a dedicated charger
