## Segment Notes

- **Trucks**: The dealership emphasizes F-150, Super Duty, Maverick and Ranger, plus chassis cab and E-Series offerings.
- **SUVs**: Explorer and Bronco/Bronco Sport are top inventory segments; Expedition and Expedition MAX cover full-size buyers.
- **Electric**: F-150 Lightning, Mustang Mach-E and E-Transit cover the EV market.
- **Commercial**: Transit, Transit Connect, E-Transit and E-Series vans support the work-truck and fleet business, including the Mullinax commercial fleet site at mullinaxfordfleet.com.
