# Friendly Lincoln

Friendly Lincoln is a Lincoln luxury vehicle dealership located at 2800 N Telegraph Rd, Monroe, MI 48162. The dealership serves Monroe County and the greater Detroit-Toledo area, offering new and pre-owned Lincoln sales, a factory-trained service center, a collision center, genuine OEM parts, and Lincoln Pickup & Delivery.
