# Friendly Lincoln Used Vehicles

Friendly Lincoln sells pre-owned and Lincoln Certified Pre-Owned vehicles alongside its new inventory. Because the dealership shares the Friendly Auto Group campus with Friendly Ford, the pre-owned lot carries a substantial number of Ford models in addition to Lincolns.

## Inventory Composition

A sample of the vehicle feed (page 1 of 20) showed:

- **16 used**, **3 certified pre-owned**, and **1 new** vehicle
- **Makes**: Ford (16) and Lincoln (4)
- **Ford models**: F-150, Escape, Edge, Explorer, Maverick, Bronco Sport, Ranger
- **Lincoln models**: Corsair (2 CPO), Aviator (1), Nautilus (1 CPO)
- **Pre-owned price range** (sample): $23,154 to $50,154
- **Year range** (sample): 2021 to 2026; mileage 2,408 to 100,711 miles

## Certified Pre-Owned

Lincoln Certified Pre-Owned vehicles pass a factory-backed inspection and carry extended warranty coverage and roadside assistance. CPO Corsair and Nautilus models were among the inventory sampled.
