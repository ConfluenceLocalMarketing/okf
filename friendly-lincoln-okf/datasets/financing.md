# Friendly Lincoln Financing

Friendly Lincoln's finance center offers lease and loan options through Lincoln Automotive Financial Services (Lincoln AFS). The finance team supports both purchasing and leasing a new or pre-owned Lincoln.

## Financing Programs

- **Purchase a Lincoln** - Auto loans with flexible terms and competitive APRs.
- **Lease a Lincoln** - Red Carpet Lease options through Lincoln AFS.
- **Flex Buy** - Alternative ownership structure with a final payment option.
- **GAP Coverage** - Optional coverage for the gap between loan balance and vehicle value.
