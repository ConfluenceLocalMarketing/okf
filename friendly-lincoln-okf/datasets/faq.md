# Friendly Lincoln FAQ

Common questions and answers about Friendly Lincoln's sales, financing, service, and dealership programs. Synthesized from the dealership's published prompt set and website content (synthesized).

## Buying

**Does Friendly Lincoln offer a money-back guarantee?**
Yes. Friendly Lincoln advertises a Money Back Guarantee on vehicle purchases, giving customers confidence in their buying decision.

**Can I buy a Lincoln online?**
Yes. Friendly Lincoln supports a fully online buying process, including virtual video walkarounds, test drive at home, digital financing, and home delivery.

**What is the trade-in process?**
Friendly Lincoln offers trade-in appraisal with instant offers, and the value can be applied toward a new Lincoln purchase.

## Financing

**Does Friendly Lincoln offer 0% APR financing?**
Yes, 0% APR financing for 48 months is available on select 2026 Lincoln Aviator and Nautilus models during the Summer Sales Event.

**What financing programs are available?**
Lease and loan options through Lincoln Automotive Financial Services, including Red Carpet Lease, Flex Buy, and GAP Coverage.

## Service

**Does Friendly Lincoln offer pickup and delivery for service?**
Yes. Lincoln Pickup & Delivery provides complimentary pickup, delivery, and a loaner for owners of 2017 and newer model-year Lincoln vehicles.

**What services does the service center provide?**
Oil changes, brake service, battery service, tire care, general service, and genuine OEM parts and accessories, plus a collision center for body work.
