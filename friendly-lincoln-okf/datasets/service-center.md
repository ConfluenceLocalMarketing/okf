# Friendly Lincoln Service Center

Friendly Lincoln operates a factory-trained Lincoln service and parts center and a collision (body shop) center at 2800 N Telegraph Rd, Monroe, MI. The service department uses OEM-approved parts and is staffed by certified technicians.

## Services Offered

- **General Service** - Routine maintenance and repair
- **Oil Change** - Factory-specified oil and filter service
- **Brake Service** - Brake inspection and repair
- **Battery Service** - Battery testing and replacement
- **Tire Care** - Tire rotation, balancing, and replacement
- **Accessories** - Genuine Lincoln accessories via accessories.lincoln.com

## Lincoln Pickup & Delivery

Complimentary pickup, delivery, and a loaner Lincoln on your schedule. Valid for owners of 2017 and newer model-year Lincoln vehicles; available for retail and warranty repairs.

## Collision Center

A dedicated collision center provides body shop services, staffed by a Collision Center Manager, an Estimator, and a Body Shop Administrative Assistant.
