# Friendly Lincoln

An Open Knowledge Format (OKF) v0.1 bundle describing Friendly Lincoln, a Lincoln luxury vehicle dealership at 2800 N Telegraph Rd, Monroe, MI 48162 serving Monroe County and the greater Detroit-Toledo area. It covers the dealership profile, new and pre-owned vehicle sales, service, collision center, financing, staff, reviews, the Money Back Guarantee, and AI-optimized discovery endpoints.

## Datasets

- [dealership.md](https://promptgraph.nyc3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/OKF/friendly-lincoln/datasets/dealership.md) - Lincoln luxury vehicle dealership in Monroe, MI serving Monroe County and the greater Detroit-Toledo area.
- [new-vehicles.md](https://promptgraph.nyc3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/OKF/friendly-lincoln/datasets/new-vehicles.md) - New Lincoln SUV inventory spanning the 2026 Corsair, Nautilus, Aviator, Navigator, and Navigator L.
- [used-vehicles.md](https://promptgraph.nyc3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/OKF/friendly-lincoln/datasets/used-vehicles.md) - Pre-owned and Lincoln Certified Pre-Owned inventory, including Ford models from the affiliated Friendly Ford store.
- [service-center.md](https://promptgraph.nyc3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/OKF/friendly-lincoln/datasets/service-center.md) - Factory-trained service center and collision center with OEM parts and Lincoln Pickup & Delivery.
- [staff.md](https://promptgraph.nyc3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/OKF/friendly-lincoln/datasets/staff.md) - Staff directory covering management, sales, service, collision, parts, and office departments.
- [faq.md](https://promptgraph.nyc3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/OKF/friendly-lincoln/datasets/faq.md) - Common questions about buying, financing, trading in, and servicing Lincoln vehicles.
- [financing.md](https://promptgraph.nyc3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/OKF/friendly-lincoln/datasets/financing.md) - Financing and leasing through Lincoln Automotive Financial Services with online credit application.
