# Hyundai of Central Florida

## Overview

Hyundai of Central Florida is a full-line Hyundai dealership located at 17325 East Highway 50, Clermont, FL 34711, serving Clermont, Lake County, and the broader Central Florida market. It sells new, used, and certified pre-owned sedans, SUVs, and hatchbacks, and promotes Up Front Pricing with no dealer fees. The store includes sales, internet sales, finance, service, and parts departments.
