# Used Vehicles - Hyundai of Central Florida

## Overview

Hyundai of Central Florida maintains a selection of pre-owned vehicles at /used-vehicles/. The used inventory includes Hyundai models alongside other brands, with a certified pre-owned program and TradePending-powered valuation tools on the search page.

## Used Hyundai Selection

- **Subcompact sedan**: Accent
- **Compact sedans**: Elantra (larger option for small-car buyers)
- **Midsize sedan**: Sonata (more spacious interior)
- **Subcompact SUVs**: Venue (budget-friendly), Kona (fun to drive)
- **Compact SUV**: Tucson
- **Midsize SUVs**: Santa Fe (more room), Palisade (3 rows, seats 7-8)
- **Compact truck**: Santa Cruz (sporty, adventurous pickup)

## Used Vehicles from Other Brands

- **Sedans**: Honda Accord, Toyota Corolla, Ford Focus
- **SUVs**: Jeep Compass and Wrangler, Toyota RAV4, Honda CR-V
- **Trucks**: Ford F-150, Chevy Silverado 1500, Toyota Tacoma

## Buying Tools

- **Value Your Trade**: TradePending online trade-in valuation.
- **What's my buying power?**: Estimate the ideal purchase price from a desired monthly payment in under a minute.
- **Inventory search**: Voice-search enabled with filter links (make, model, year).
- **Sell your car**: Cash purchase offer for a used car without buying a new one via /sell-your-used-car/.

## Certified Pre-Owned

- Hyundai certified pre-owned vehicles with warranty coverage near Orlando, FL are promoted in the dealership's prompt library.
- Used vehicles are also available for cash purchase with trade-in valuation applied.
