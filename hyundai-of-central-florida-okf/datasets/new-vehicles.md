# New Vehicles - Hyundai of Central Florida

## Overview

Hyundai of Central Florida sells the full new Hyundai lineup, covering subcompact and compact cars, sedans, SUVs and crossovers, electrified models, and the Santa Cruz compact truck. New vehicles are listed at /new-vehicles/ and /new-hyundai-vehicles/, with model-specific landing pages for each nameplate.

## Model Lineup

Model pages exist for the following nameplates on the dealership website:

- **Sedans**: Accent, Elantra, Elantra Hybrid, Elantra N, Sonata, Sonata Hybrid
- **Electrified sedans**: IONIQ 6 (all-electric streamliner)
- **SUVs / crossovers**: Venue, Kona, Kona N, Tucson, Tucson Hybrid, Tucson Plug-in Hybrid, Santa Fe, Santa Fe Hybrid, Palisade, Palisade Hybrid
- **Electric SUVs**: IONIQ 5, IONIQ 9 (3-row)
- **Performance**: Elantra N, Kona N, IONIQ 5 N

See [model-comparison.md](https://promptgraph.nyc3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/OKF/hyundai-of-central-florida/tables/model-comparison.md) for a side-by-side comparison of key models.

## Buying Channels

- **In-store**: Browse and test drive at 17325 East Highway 50, Clermont, FL 34711.
- **Express Store**: Online purchase experience at https://express.hyundaicfl.com/inventory.
- **Value Your Trade**: TradePending trade-in valuation widget on the inventory search page.
- **Buying power calculator**: Monthly-budget tool to estimate purchase price.
- **Search inventory**: Voice-search enabled inventory search with filter links per model and year.
