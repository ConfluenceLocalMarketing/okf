# Financing & Auto Loans - Hyundai of Central Florida

## Overview

The Hyundai of Central Florida finance center arranges financing and leasing for new and used trucks, cars, and SUVs. Customers can complete a secure finance application online and work with the finance team in-store.

## Finance Center

- **Phone**: 888-566-5820 (Main / Sales)
- **Location**: 17325 East Highway 50, Clermont, FL 34711
- **Sales hours**: Mon-Fri 9:00a-8:00p, Sat 9:00a-7:00p, Sun 12:00p-6:00p
- Online application: https://www.hyundaicfl.com/finance/apply-for-financing/

## Online Credit Application

- Secure finance application available at /finance/apply-for-financing/.
- Finance team guides customers through the process and arranges approval.

## Financing Programs

- **New vehicle financing** - manufacturer rates via Hyundai Motor Finance, including current APR specials.
- **Used vehicle financing** - competitive rates on pre-owned inventory.
- **Leasing** - new car leasing programs.
- **Flexible financing options** - promoted in the prompt library for buyers near Clermont and Orlando.
