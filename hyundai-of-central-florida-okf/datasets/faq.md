# Frequently Asked Questions - Hyundai of Central Florida

## Overview

Answers to common questions about Hyundai of Central Florida, based on the dealership website, the Google Business Profile, and the PromptGraph prompt library. Where an answer is not directly published by the dealership, it is flagged as synthesized.

## Up Front Pricing & Fees

**Do you charge dealer fees?**
No. The dealership promotes Up Front Pricing with no dealer fees; the advertised price is the price you pay plus tax and tag. (synthesized detail from the GBP description "Up Front Pricing and no dealer fees")

## Financing

**How do I get pre-approved for an auto loan?**
Use the secure online finance application at /finance/apply-for-financing/ or contact the finance team. The dealership offers competitive rates and terms on new and used vehicles. See [financing.md](https://promptgraph.nyc3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/OKF/hyundai-of-central-florida/datasets/financing.md).

**What APR specials are available on new vehicles?**
Current offers (expiring 2026-08-31) include 0% APR for 60 months on the 2026 Tucson, Santa Fe, and Santa Fe Hybrid; 0% APR for 72 months on the 2026 IONIQ 5 and IONIQ 9; 0.99% APR on the 2026 Sonata; and 3.49%-3.99% APR on the 2026 Elantra, Kona, and Palisade. See [new-vehicles.md](https://promptgraph.nyc3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/OKF/hyundai-of-central-florida/datasets/new-vehicles.md).

**Do you offer leasing?**
Yes. The dealership offers new car leasing as one of its Google Business Profile service categories.

## Trade-In & Selling

**How do I value my trade-in?**
Use the TradePending "Value Your Trade" widget on the inventory search page or visit /sell-your-used-car/. The dealership buys used cars for cash without requiring a new purchase.

## Inventory

**What new Hyundai models do you carry?**
The full lineup including Accent, Elantra, Sonata, Venue, Kona, Tucson, Santa Fe, Palisade, Santa Cruz, IONIQ 5, IONIQ 6, and IONIQ 9, plus hybrid and N performance variants. See [new-vehicles.md](https://promptgraph.nyc3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/OKF/hyundai-of-central-florida/datasets/new-vehicles.md).

**Do you sell used vehicles from other brands?**
Yes. Used inventory includes Honda, Toyota, Ford, Jeep, and Chevrolet models in addition to used Hyundais. See [used-vehicles.md](https://promptgraph.nyc3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/OKF/hyundai-of-central-florida/datasets/used-vehicles.md).

## Service

**Can I buy online and get delivery?**
The Express Store (https://express.hyundaicfl.com/inventory) supports online buying. Home delivery availability is promoted in the prompt library ("Online Car Buying & Delivery").
