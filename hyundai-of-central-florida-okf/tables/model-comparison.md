## Segment Notes

- **Sedans**: Accent, Elantra (including Elantra N and Elantra Hybrid), Sonata (including Sonata Hybrid), and the all-electric IONIQ 6.
- **SUVs / crossovers**: Venue, Kona, Tucson, Santa Fe, and Palisade, with hybrid and plug-in hybrid variants for Tucson and Santa Fe, plus the IONIQ 5 and 3-row IONIQ 9 electric SUVs.
- **Performance**: Elantra N, Kona N, and IONIQ 5 N.
- **Truck**: Santa Cruz compact sport-adventure pickup.

## Electrified Options

- **Hybrid**: Elantra Hybrid, Sonata Hybrid, Tucson Hybrid, Santa Fe Hybrid, Palisade Hybrid
- **Plug-in hybrid**: Tucson Plug-in Hybrid
- **All-electric**: IONIQ 5, IONIQ 5 N, IONIQ 6, IONIQ 9
