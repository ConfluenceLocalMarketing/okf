## Segment Notes

- **Trucks**: The dealership emphasizes F-150, Super Duty, F-650 / F-750, Maverick, and Ranger, plus commercial Transit vans through its commercial/work-truck department.
- **SUVs**: Explorer and Bronco/Bronco Sport are top inventory segments; Expedition and Expedition MAX cover full-size buyers.
- **Electric**: F-150 Lightning, Mustang Mach-E, and E-Transit cover the EV market.
