# Service Center and Quick Lane

Mullinax Ford of Kissimmee operates a full service center staffed by factory-trained technicians using genuine Ford parts, plus a Quick Lane Tire and Auto Center for fast maintenance on all makes and models.

## Service Center

- Factory-trained Ford technicians
- Genuine Ford and Motorcraft parts
- Services for Ford owners can be scheduled at [schedule-service](https://www.mullinaxfordkiss.com/service/schedule-service/)
- Service coupons and specials at [service-specials](https://www.mullinaxfordkiss.com/service/service-specials/)

## Quick Lane Tire and Auto Center

Quick Lane at Mullinax Ford Kissimmee provides maintenance and repair services for all vehicle makes and models, with 8 service bays.

### Quick Lane Services

- Synthetic and synthetic blend oil changes with oil filter replacement
- Battery testing, charging and new battery installation
- Tire rotation and balancing, flat tire repairs and new tire installation
- Brake pad replacement, rotor and caliper repair, brake line repair
- Air filter replacement
- Windshield wiper replacement
- Belt and hose replacement
- Wheel alignment
- Spark plug replacement
- Electrical repair

### Quick Lane Highlights

- 8 service bays for faster service
- Speed and timing
- Low tire price guarantee
- Genuine Motorcraft parts
- Lifetime warranty on brakes
- Services all makes and models
- Comfortable waiting area with refreshments and free WiFi

### Service Facts

- Oil changes generally recommended every 5,000 to 10,000 miles depending on vehicle
- Tires can last 30,000 to 60,000 miles; replace when tread wears to 3/32 inch (2.4 mm) or less
- Brakes can last 30,000 to 60,000 miles; brake pad replacement roughly $190 per axle
- Typical car battery capacity is 12.6 volts with 300 to 760 CCA; in Florida batteries typically last two to three years
