# Financing

Mullinax Ford of Kissimmee offers vehicle financing and leasing through Ford Credit and partner lenders, with online tools for pre-approval, payments and trade-in valuation.

## Financing Tools

- Apply for financing online at [apply-for-financing](https://www.mullinaxfordkiss.com/finance/apply-for-financing/)
- Payment calculator at [payment-calculator](https://www.mullinaxfordkiss.com/payment-calculator/)
- Trade-in valuation available through the dealership website

## Services

- New and used vehicle financing
- Leasing through Ford Credit
- Pre-approval before visiting the dealership
- Financing for all credit situations via partner lenders
