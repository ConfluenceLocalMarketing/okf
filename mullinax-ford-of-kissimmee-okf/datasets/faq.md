# Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the dealership, custom factory orders and the buying process at Mullinax Ford of Kissimmee.

## Custom Factory Order FAQ

### How long does a custom factory order take?

Lead times vary by model and configuration. The sales team provides a delivery estimate when the order is placed.

### Does Ford charge extra for a custom factory order?

Ford does not charge extra for customer-ordered vehicles. Pricing follows the same Up Front Pricing model.

### Can I use incentives on a factory order?

Incentives can be applied at order placement or at final delivery, whichever the customer prefers, subject to current program rules.

### How do I track my ordered vehicle?

Once a VIN is assigned, the vehicle can be tracked at [shop.ford.com/vehicleordertracking](https://www.ford.com/help/buying/vehicle-ordering/). The dealership contacts the customer when the vehicle arrives.

## Dealership FAQ

### Is there a dealer fee?

No. Mullinax Ford of Kissimmee charges a $0 dealer fee. The advertised price is the price you pay.

### Does the dealership negotiate?

No. The dealership uses Up Front Pricing, a no-haggle model where the sticker price is the final price.

### What does the dealership service?

The service center handles all Ford makes and models, and Quick Lane services all vehicle makes and models.

### What areas does the dealership serve?

The dealership serves Kissimmee, Poinciana, South Orlando and St. Cloud.
