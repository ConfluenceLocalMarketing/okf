# Used Vehicles

Mullinax Ford of Kissimmee sells used vehicles of all makes and models alongside its new Ford inventory. The homepage inventory counter displayed 1,288 used vehicles out of 3,534 total.

## Used Inventory

- 1,288 used vehicles listed on the homepage inventory counter
- Used vehicles include Fords as well as other makes and models
- Dedicated listings: Vehicles Under 10K at [priced-under-10k](https://www.mullinaxfordkiss.com/used-vehicles/priced-under-10k/)
- Used vehicles are subject to prior sale

## Ford Certified Pre-Owned

- 914 Certified Pre-Owned Ford vehicles listed
- Program page: [certified-pre-owned-vehicles](https://www.mullinaxfordkiss.com/used-vehicles/certified-pre-owned-vehicles/)
- Ford CPO vehicles are inspected and covered by the Ford Blue Advantage limited warranty program

## Buying

- Up Front Pricing with no dealer fees
- Trade-in valuation available on the dealership website
- Financing and pre-approval through the [financing page](https://www.mullinaxfordkiss.com/finance/)
- Schedule a test drive online
