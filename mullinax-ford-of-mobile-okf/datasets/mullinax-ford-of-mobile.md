## Up Front Pricing

- No dealer fees on any vehicle. The Up Front Price is the price you pay, plus only tax and title.
- No electronic filing fee; the price includes $0 dealer fees and $0 electronic filing fee.
- Pricing is based on market research, with no back-and-forth negotiation required.
- The dealership's volume of inventory allows it to pass on warehouse-club-style pricing to consumers.
