# Financing & Auto Loans - Mullinax Ford of Mobile

## Overview

The finance department at Mullinax Ford of Mobile arranges new and used vehicle financing. Customers can begin the credit application online and work with finance staff in-store. The finance team maintains close relations with top lenders and aggressively pursues the best possible rate and terms for the lowest possible payment.

## Online Credit Application

- Apply online at https://www.mullinaxfordmobile.com/apply-online
- Secure credit application; a completed application triggers a prompt follow-up call.
- Loan analysts provide a preliminary look at each customer's situation.

## Financing Programs

- **New vehicle financing** - financing on any new Ford vehicle for sale.
- **Used vehicle financing** - competitive rates on pre-owned inventory.
- **No money down** - zero-down options with no payments for 30 to 60 days.
- **Bad credit financing** - programs for buyers with less-than-perfect credit who may have been turned down elsewhere.
- **Leasing** - Ford lease programs (referenced in promotions such as F-150 lease deals).

## Lending Approach

- Close relations with top lenders.
- Financing is handled professionally and promptly.
- The finance team treats each customer's whole situation rather than just a credit number.

## What to Bring

- Driver's license and insurance card for everyone signing on the loan.
- Proof of residence (e.g. phone or electric bill) if unsure about credit.
- Most recent paycheck stubs.
- Down payment and the title or registration of any trade-in.

## Tools

- **Trade-in valuation**: online appraisal applied toward any purchase; see [used-vehicles.md](https://promptgraph.nyc3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/OKF/mullinax-ford-of-mobile/datasets/used-vehicles.md).
- **Value your trade**: combine trade value with financing to reduce the financed amount.
