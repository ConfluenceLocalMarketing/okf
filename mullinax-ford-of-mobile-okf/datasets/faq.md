# Frequently Asked Questions - Mullinax Ford of Mobile

## Overview

Answers to common customer questions published on the dealership website, covering financing, pricing, and general dealership topics.

## Financing Questions

- **Q: Can I get financed with no money down?**
  A: Yes. Customers can get financed with no money out of pocket and no payments for 30 to 60 days. Contact the finance team or start the online credit application at https://www.mullinaxfordmobile.com/apply-online.

- **Q: What do I need to bring to be sure I have everything?**
  A: Everyone who is signing on the loan should bring a driver's license and insurance card. If unsure about credit, bring proof of residence (a phone or electric bill). Bring most recent paycheck stubs, down payment, and the title or registration of your trade-in.

- **Q: What if I do not have very good credit?**
  A: The dealership looks at the whole situation rather than just a credit number and aims to work with customers who have been turned down elsewhere. Contact the finance team at https://www.mullinaxfordmobile.com/apply-online.

- **Q: How does financing work?**
  A: The finance department maintains close relations with top lenders and pursues the best possible rate and terms, ensuring the lowest possible payment for the next vehicle purchase.

## Pricing Questions

- **Q: Do you charge dealer fees?**
  A: No. Mullinax Up Front Pricing means no dealer fees on any vehicle, and no electronic filing fee - price plus tax and title only.

- **Q: What is the Up Front Price based on?**
  A: The dealership researches market pricing and positions it lower, avoids negotiation, charges no dealer fee, and relies on inventory volume to pass warehouse-club-style pricing to consumers. See the Up Front Pricing page: https://www.mullinaxfordmobile.com/up-front-pricing

## Dealership Questions

- **Q: Where is the dealership located?**
  A: 7311 Airport Blvd, Mobile, AL 36608, serving Mobile and the surrounding South Alabama market.

- **Q: What are the dealership's hours?**
  A: Sales: Mon-Fri 9:00a-8:00p, Sat 9:00a-6:00p, Sun Closed. Service and parts: Mon-Fri 7:30a-6:00p, Sat 8:00a-4:00p, Sun Closed. See [hours.md](https://promptgraph.nyc3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/OKF/mullinax-ford-of-mobile/tables/hours.md) for all departments.

- **Q: Do you sell work trucks / commercial vehicles?**
  A: Yes. A Commercial / Fleet department handles work trucks and commercial vehicles via mullinaxford.com/commercial-department and mullinaxfordfleet.com.
