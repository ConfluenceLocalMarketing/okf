# Service Center & Body Shop - Mullinax Ford of Mobile

## Overview

The Mullinax Ford of Mobile service department performs maintenance and repair using Ford-trained technicians and original equipment manufacturer (OEM) Ford parts. A body shop handles collision repair for any make or model.

## Service Department

- **Hours**: Mon-Fri 7:30a-6:00p, Sat 8:00a-4:00p, Sun Closed
- **Phone**: (888) 871-9465
- **Address**: 7311 Airport Blvd, Mobile, AL 36608
- Ford-trained technicians
- Ford genuine / OEM parts and accessories (parts department: (888) 943-1825)
- Online appointment scheduling: https://www.mullinaxfordmobile.com/car-repair

## Services Offered

- **Oil changes** - conventional and synthetic, oil filter replacement; 5w20 and 5w30 semi-synthetic are the most common grades
- **Battery replacement & charging** - testing, new battery installation, charging (batteries in the Gulf Coast climate typically last two to three years)
- **Tire repair & replacement** - rotation and balancing, flat tire repairs, new tire installation
- **Brake repair & replacement** - pad replacement, rotor and caliper repair and replacement, brake line repair
- **Air conditioning repair** - general repair, cabin filter replacement, refrigerant recharging
- **Other maintenance** - windshield wipers, belts and hoses, wheel alignment, spark plugs, electrical repair, transmission repair, exhaust repair, steering and suspension, window glass, interior upholstery and headliner repair
