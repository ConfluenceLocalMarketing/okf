# Pre-Owned Vehicles - Mullinax Ford of Mobile

## Overview

Mullinax Ford of Mobile carries a pre-owned inventory spanning Ford and all makes and models. Used vehicles are priced with the same Up Front Pricing policy as new vehicles: $0 dealer fees, $0 electronic filing fee, and a transparent Up Front Price.

## Used Inventory

- **Used SUVs** - Explorer, Escape, Edge, Bronco Sport, and other makes.
- **Used trucks** - F-150 and Super Duty, plus non-Ford trucks.
- **All makes and models** - Ford, Chevrolet, GMC, Toyota, Honda, Nissan, Hyundai, Kia, Jeep, Volkswagen, Mercedes-Benz, and more.
- **Certified vehicles** - certified pre-owned units flagged in the inventory (e.g. certified Nissan Altima, Volkswagen Passat, Hyundai Tucson, Cadillac XT5).
- **JD Power reference pricing** - select units display a JD Power value with a "You Save" comparison against the Up Front Price.

## Pricing Model

Listings display a pricing breakdown including:

- JD Power reference price (where applicable)
- You Save (difference from JD Power reference, where applicable)
- Dealer Fees: $0
- Electronic Filing Fee: $0
- Up Front Price

## Selling & Trading

- **Sell your car for cash**: The dealership purchases used vehicles outright; see https://www.mullinaxfordmobile.com/sell-vehicle-for-cash
- **Trade-in valuation**: Online trade appraisal applied to any purchase.
- **Trade-in offers**: Combine trade value with financing through the finance center.
