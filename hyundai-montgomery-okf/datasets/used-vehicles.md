# Used Cars, Trucks & SUVs - Hyundai Montgomery

Hyundai Montgomery is a used car headquarters with one of the largest selections of pre-owned vehicles in the area. The dealership website listed 319 used and certified used vehicles in Montgomery, AL.

## Inventory Scope

- **Total vehicles:** 319 used and certified used vehicles
- **Multi-make selection** including Hyundai, Jeep, Ford, Toyota, Buick, Cadillac, Audi, BMW, Ram, Nissan, Honda, Volkswagen, and Mercedes-Benz
- **Vehicle types:** Sedans, SUVs, trucks, and crossovers
- **Hyundai models:** Popular sedans like the Elantra and Sonata, plus SUVs like the Tucson, Palisade, and Kona

## Quality Assurance

- **Vetting process** - Every used vehicle must meet or exceed dealership quality standards before earning a place in inventory
- **Hyundai Certified Pre-Owned (CPO)** - Certified used vehicles with warranty coverage
- **Carfax History Reports** - Available on request

## Popular Used Models

- **Hyundai** - Elantra, Sonata, Tucson, Palisade, Kona

- **Toyota** - Tacoma, 4Runner
- **Premium** - Buick, Cadillac, Audi, BMW
