# Financing & Leasing - Hyundai Montgomery

The Hyundai Montgomery Finance Center offers purchase financing and lease programs for new and pre-owned vehicles.

## Financing Options

- **Online Application** - Secure online credit application at /finance/apply-for-financing/
- **Multiple Lending Partners** - The finance team works with many lenders to find competitive interest rates for every buyer
- **Flexible Terms** - Loan and lease solutions for different ownership goals and budgets
- **Customizable Terms** - Various lease options and purchase terms
- **Competitive APR Specials** - Manufacturer incentives through Hyundai Motor Finance

## Leasing

Leasing is available for customers who prefer lower monthly payments and the option to drive a new vehicle every few years. New vehicle lease deals include the 2026 Tucson.

## Buying vs. Leasing

| Factor | Buying | Leasing |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly Payment | Higher | Lower |
| Ownership | Own the vehicle at end of term | Return vehicle at end of term |
| Mileage | Unlimited | Limited by contract |
| Sales Tax | Paid upfront or rolled into loan | Included in monthly payment |

## Trade-In
