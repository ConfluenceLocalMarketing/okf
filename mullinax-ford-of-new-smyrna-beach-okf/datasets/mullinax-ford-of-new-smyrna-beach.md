# Mullinax Ford of New Smyrna Beach

## Overview

Mullinax Ford of New Smyrna Beach is a full-line Ford dealership located at 2317 S.R. 44, New Smyrna Beach, FL 32168, serving New Smyrna Beach, Daytona Beach, Port Orange, Edgewater, Deltona, Sanford, and Palm Coast along the Central Florida coast. It is part of the family-owned Mullinax Ford group (family run since 1970) and is a no-dealer-fee dealership that markets Up Front Pricing on every vehicle.

## Up Front Pricing

- No dealer fees, no doc fee, and no haggling on any new or used vehicle.
- Price you see is the price you pay, plus only tax and title.
- The Up Front Price includes Mullinax Discounts, Factory Rebates, and Ford Credit Financing Rebates; other rebates (College, Military, Private) may apply in addition.
- The dealership publishes comparison tables showing dealer fees of competing area dealerships ranging up to $3,992 to illustrate customer savings.
