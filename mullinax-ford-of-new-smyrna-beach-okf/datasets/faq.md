# Frequently Asked Questions - Mullinax Ford of New Smyrna Beach

## Overview

Answers to common customer questions published on the dealership website and derived from dealership pages, covering pricing, location, hours, service, and general dealership topics.

## Pricing Questions

- **Q: Do you charge dealer fees?**
  A: No. Mullinax Ford of New Smyrna Beach is a no-dealer-fee dealership. The dealership's no-fee policy covers no doc fee, no dealer fee, and no haggle. Customers pay the Up Front Price plus only tax and title.

- **Q: What is Up Front Pricing?**
  A: Every vehicle is marked with a transparent Up Front Price on the windshield and online. The price includes Mullinax Discounts, Factory Rebates, and Ford Credit Financing Rebates, and may include Trade Assist, Business Owner Rebate, or Owner Loyalty Rebate when available. Additional rebates (College, Military, Private) may apply.

- **Q: How much do competing dealerships charge in dealer fees?**
  A: The dealership publishes comparison data showing dealer fees at competing Central Florida dealerships ranging from about $399 to $3,992 per vehicle, illustrating savings at a no-fee store.

## Service Questions

- **Q: Do you service all makes and models?**
  A: The Quick Lane provides express maintenance for all makes and models with 8 service bays. The main service center focuses on Ford vehicles with factory-trained technicians.

- **Q: Do you offer mobile service or pickup and delivery?**
  A: Yes. Mobile service brings Ford-trained technicians to a customer's location, and pickup & delivery service is available for the service center.

- **Q: Can I schedule service online?**
  A: Yes, at https://www.mullinaxfordnsb.com/service-appointment/. Service specials are at https://www.mullinaxfordnsb.com/service-specials/.

## Financing Questions

- **Q: How can I get pre-approved for financing?**
  A: Submit an online credit application through the finance center at https://www.mullinaxfordnsb.com/finance/apply-for-financing/ or work with a finance manager in-store.

- **Q: Can I estimate my monthly payment?**
  A: Yes, using the payment calculator at https://www.mullinaxfordnsb.com/payment-calculator/.

- **Q: Can I deduct a work vehicle purchase?**
  A: The dealership provides IRS Section 179 deduction information at https://www.mullinaxfordnsb.com/irs-section-179-vehicles-deduction-new-smyrna-beach-fl/.

## General Questions
