# Financing & Auto Loans - Mullinax Ford of New Smyrna Beach

## Overview

The finance department at Mullinax Ford of New Smyrna Beach arranges new and used vehicle financing and leasing. Customers can begin the credit application online and work with finance staff in-store. Finance managers are listed in [staff.md](https://promptgraph.nyc3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/OKF/mullinax-ford-of-new-smyrna-beach/datasets/staff.md).

## Online Credit Application

- Apply online at https://www.mullinaxfordnsb.com/finance/apply-for-financing/
- Fast pre-qualification for an auto loan.
- Applications are submitted to lending partners for competitive rates.

## Financing Programs

- **New vehicle financing** - manufacturer rates and programs on new Ford vehicles.
- **Used vehicle financing** - competitive rates on pre-owned inventory.
- **Leasing** - Ford lease programs with flexible mileage terms.
- **Ford Credit Financing Rebates** - included in the Up Front Price where applicable.
- **Trade-in financing** - combine trade value with financing to reduce the financed amount.

## Business & Work Vehicle Financing

- **IRS Section 179 deduction**: Guidance for deducting qualifying work vehicles is published at https://www.mullinaxfordnsb.com/irs-section-179-vehicles-deduction-new-smyrna-beach-fl/.
- Commercial and work-truck financing is handled through the Commercial department (see [staff.md](https://promptgraph.nyc3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/OKF/mullinax-ford-of-new-smyrna-beach/datasets/staff.md)).

## Tools

- **Payment calculator**: estimate monthly payments by price, down payment, and term at https://www.mullinaxfordnsb.com/payment-calculator/.
- **Trade-in valuation**: online appraisal at https://www.mullinaxfordnsb.com/value-your-trade/ applied toward any purchase; see [used-vehicles.md](https://promptgraph.nyc3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/OKF/mullinax-ford-of-new-smyrna-beach/datasets/used-vehicles.md).
