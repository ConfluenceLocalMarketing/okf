## Segment Notes

- **Sedans**: CT4 and CT5 (plus V-Series variants) cover the luxury sport sedan segment.
- **SUVs**: XT4, XT5, and XT6 cover the compact through 3-row luxury crossover segments; Escalade and Escalade ESV cover full-size buyers.
- **Electric**: LYRIQ, OPTIQ, VISTIQ, and Escalade IQ cover the growing EV luxury market, supported by Cadillac Financial specials.
