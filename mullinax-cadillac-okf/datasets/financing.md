# Financing & Auto Loans - Mullinax Cadillac

## Overview

The finance center at Mullinax Cadillac arranges new and used vehicle financing and leasing. Customers can begin the credit application online and complete the process in-store or through the finance team. The center aims to make the process "simple and hassle-free" and to match every shopper with a payment plan that fits their life.

## Finance Center

- **Page**: https://www.mullinaxcadillac.com/finance/
- Lease or finance on new and pre-owned vehicles
- Online finance application: https://www.mullinaxcadillac.com/finance/apply-for-financing/
- Payment calculator: https://www.mullinaxcadillac.com/payment-calculator/
- Value your trade: https://www.mullinaxcadillac.com/value-your-trade/

## Financing Programs

- **New vehicle financing** - financing on any new Cadillac vehicle.
- **Used vehicle financing** - competitive rates on pre-owned inventory.
- **Leasing** - Cadillac Financial lease programs (see current lease specials on the LYRIQ and OPTIQ in [new-vehicles.md](https://promptgraph.nyc3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/OKF/mullinax-cadillac/datasets/new-vehicles.md)).
- **Pre-approval** - online credit application and pre-approval for luxury vehicle financing; see [prompts.md](https://promptgraph.nyc3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/OKF/mullinax-cadillac/references/prompts.md) prompt #4 (Auto Loan Pre-Approval).
- **Flexible financing** - flexible loan options and low APR financing (see prompt #9, Flexible Cadillac Financing).

## Trade-In

- **Value your trade**: online trade appraisal at https://www.mullinaxcadillac.com/value-your-trade/
- **Trade-in valuation** applied toward any purchase; see [used-vehicles.md](https://promptgraph.nyc3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/OKF/mullinax-cadillac/datasets/used-vehicles.md).
- **What's My Buying Power**: monthly-budget buying power tool on the used-vehicle page.

## Up Front Pricing

The finance center emphasizes that Up Front Pricing means "you never have to worry about hidden fees or haggling. When you shop with us at Mullinax Cadillac, you can shop with confidence."
