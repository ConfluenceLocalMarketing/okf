# Service Center & Parts - Mullinax Cadillac

## Overview

The Mullinax Cadillac service department performs maintenance and repair on Cadillac vehicles, and the parts department supplies genuine OEM parts for DIY projects, dealership repairs, and vehicle accessorizing.

## Service Department

- **Hours**: Mon-Fri 7:00a-6:00p, Sat 7:00a-3:00p, Sun Closed
- **Phone**: (334) 910-3470
- **Address**: 833 Eastern Blvd, Montgomery, AL 36117
- Factory-trained service staff
- Online appointment scheduling: https://www.mullinaxcadillac.com/service/serviceapptform/

## Services Offered

The service center covers routine maintenance and repair including:

- Oil changes
- Battery replacement and charging
- Brake repair and replacement
- Tires - repair, rotation, and new tire installation
- General maintenance and factory-required service

Service pricing and current service specials are published on the service department page at https://www.mullinaxcadillac.com/service/.

## Parts Department

- **Hours**: Mon-Fri 7:00a-6:00p, Sat 7:00a-3:00p, Sun Closed
- **Phone**: (334) 910-3378
- Genuine OEM factory parts kept in stock for the vehicles being serviced
- Order parts online and receive a call when the order is ready for pickup
- Parts order form: https://www.mullinaxcadillac.com/parts/partsorderform/
- Parts department page: https://www.mullinaxcadillac.com/parts/
