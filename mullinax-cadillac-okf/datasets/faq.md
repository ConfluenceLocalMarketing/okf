# Frequently Asked Questions - Mullinax Cadillac

## Overview

Answers to common customer questions about Mullinax Cadillac, covering financing, pricing, and general dealership topics. Content is derived from the dealership website (finance center, new-vehicle specials, service, and hours).

## Financing Questions

- **Q: How do I apply for financing?**
  A: Start the online credit application at https://www.mullinaxcadillac.com/finance/apply-for-financing/. The finance center can also arrange pre-approval for luxury vehicle financing.

- **Q: Can I lease a vehicle?**
  A: Yes. The finance center supports both leasing and financing on new and pre-owned vehicles. Current lease specials include the 2026 LYRIQ ($539/mo for 24 months) and 2026 OPTIQ ($379/mo for 24 months); see [new-vehicles.md](https://promptgraph.nyc3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/OKF/mullinax-cadillac/datasets/new-vehicles.md).

- **Q: Can I finance a pre-owned vehicle?**
  A: Yes. Financing is available on the pre-owned inventory; see [used-vehicles.md](https://promptgraph.nyc3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/OKF/mullinax-cadillac/datasets/used-vehicles.md).

- **Q: How much will my monthly payment be?**
  A: Use the payment calculator at https://www.mullinaxcadillac.com/payment-calculator/ or the buying-power tool on the used-vehicle page.

## Pricing Questions

- **Q: Do you charge dealer fees?**
  A: No. Mullinax Up Front Pricing means no dealer fees and no haggling; the price shown online is the price you pay (plus tax and title).

- **Q: Is the online price the final price?**
  A: Yes. Up Front Pricing is displayed on every vehicle, and the finance center states that with it "you never have to worry about hidden fees or haggling."

## Dealership Questions

- **Q: Where is the dealership located?**
  A: 833 Eastern Blvd, Montgomery, AL 36117, serving Montgomery and the surrounding Central Alabama River Region.

- **Q: What are the dealership's hours?**
  A: Sales: Mon-Fri 9:00a-7:00p, Sat 9:00a-6:00p, Sun Closed. Service and parts: Mon-Fri 7:00a-6:00p, Sat 7:00a-3:00p, Sun Closed. See [hours.md](https://promptgraph.nyc3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/OKF/mullinax-cadillac/tables/hours.md) for all departments.

- **Q: Do you sell certified pre-owned vehicles?**
  A: Yes. Certified Pre-Owned Cadillac and CarBravo-certified inventory is available; see [used-vehicles.md](https://promptgraph.nyc3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/OKF/mullinax-cadillac/datasets/used-vehicles.md).

- **Q: Can I buy a vehicle online?**
  A: Yes. The dealership offers online car buying through its buying site at https://buy.mullinaxcadillac.com/ (CarBravo), and Online Car Buying is listed as a Google Business Profile service.
