# Pre-Owned Vehicles - Mullinax Cadillac

## Overview

Mullinax Cadillac carries a pre-owned inventory spanning Cadillac and all makes and models. Used vehicles are priced with the same Up Front Pricing policy as new vehicles: transparent pricing, no dealer fees, and no haggling.

## Used Inventory

The August 2026 inventory snapshot listed 196 used, certified, courtesy, and demo units at the Montgomery location, with prices ranging from about $6,900 to $107,000.

- **Used Cadillac** - XT5, XT6, Escalade, and other Cadillac models.
- **All makes and models** - Chevrolet, Buick, GMC, Ford, Toyota, Honda, Nissan, Volkswagen, Mercedes-Benz, and more.
- **Certified Pre-Owned Cadillac** - certified units flagged in the inventory with warranty coverage.
- **Courtesy and demo** - lightly used dealer units included in the used inventory.
- **CarBravo** - GM's certified used-vehicle program with online buying at https://buy.mullinaxcadillac.com/carbravo/.
