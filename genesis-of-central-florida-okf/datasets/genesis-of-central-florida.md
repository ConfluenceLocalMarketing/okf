# Genesis of Central Florida

## Overview

Genesis of Central Florida is a Genesis brand dealership located at 4500 Collina Terrace, Clermont, FL 34711, serving Clermont, Winter Garden, Ocoee, Minneola, Groveland, Leesburg, Kissimmee, Orlando, The Villages, and the broader Central Florida market. It markets itself as a veteran-owned dealership with NO DEALER FEES, Up Front Pricing, and NO DEALER MARKUP. The dealership sells new, used, and Genesis Certified Pre-Owned sedans and SUVs and operates full service and parts departments.
