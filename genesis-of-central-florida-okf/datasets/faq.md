# FAQ - Genesis of Central Florida

## Pricing & Fees

- **Do you charge dealer fees?** No. Genesis of Central Florida never charges dealer fees, dealer-installed options, or dealer markup. Buyers save an average of $900 in fees; you pay the Up Front price plus only tax and tag.
- **What is Up Front Pricing?** The best price is placed on every vehicle - new, pre-owned, and certified - with no haggling required.
- **Is the dealership veteran owned?** Yes. The dealership markets itself as proudly Veteran Owned.

## Buying & Inventory

- **Can I buy a Genesis online?** Yes. The dealership supports online car buying, quote requests, and online financing applications. See https://genesiscfl.com/genesis-online-car-buying.
- **What Genesis models are available?** The lineup includes the G70, G80, G90 sedans and the GV60, GV70, Electrified GV70, GV80, and GV80 Coupe SUVs, in new, used, and Certified Pre-Owned forms.
- **Can I test drive a GV80 near Highway 50?** Yes, the dealership is on 4500 Collina Terrace in Clermont, off the Highway 50 corridor, and offers test drive scheduling.
- **Do you carry all-wheel drive models?** Yes, extensive AWD SUV stock is available in new and pre-owned inventory.
- **What budget-friendly pre-owned options exist?** Collections include pre-owned luxury SUVs under $40,000 and inventory under $30,000 and under 50,000 miles.

## Financing & Trade-In

- **Can I get pre-approved for an auto loan?** Yes, via the online credit application at https://genesiscfl.com/finance, with flexible rates and options from top lenders and credit unions.
- **Do you offer lease options?** Yes, including offers on the GV70, Electrified GV70, and other models, plus national and regional incentives.
- **What is my vehicle worth?** Use the online trade valuation tool at https://genesiscfl.com/trade-valuation for a free estimate in under 30 seconds.
- **Can I sell my car for cash without buying?** Yes, the dealership buys vehicles for cash via https://genesiscfl.com/sell-us-your-car.

## Service

- **Do you offer pickup and delivery for service?** Yes, complimentary Genesis Service Valet picks up and returns the vehicle, with loaner vehicles available.
- **Is the car wash included?** A complimentary car wash is provided with every service.
- **Who performs the service?** Factory-trained and certified Genesis technicians using genuine OEM parts.
