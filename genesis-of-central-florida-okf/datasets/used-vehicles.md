# Used Vehicles - Genesis of Central Florida

## Overview

Genesis of Central Florida sells used and Genesis Certified Pre-Owned (CPO) vehicles. The used lot includes Genesis models as well as many other makes and models, with options to finance, trade in, or sell a vehicle for cash.
