# Financing - Genesis of Central Florida

## Overview

Genesis of Central Florida offers simplified, transparent financing with Up Front Pricing and no dealer fees. Finance experts work with top lenders and credit unions to tailor loan and lease packages to each customer's goals.

## Options

- **Online Credit Application** - apply online at https://genesiscfl.com/finance (powered by Motive).
- **Auto Loans** - customizable loan terms and competitive interest rates.
- **Leasing** - a variety of lease options, including current offers on the GV70 and Electrified GV70.
- **Bad-Credit Programs** - experience helping drivers in diverse credit circumstances.
- **Trade-In Valuation** - free online estimate at https://genesiscfl.com/trade-valuation.
- **Payment Calculator** - estimate monthly payments at https://genesiscfl.com/payment-calculator.
