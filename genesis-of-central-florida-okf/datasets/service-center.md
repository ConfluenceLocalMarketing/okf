# Service Center - Genesis of Central Florida

## Overview

The Genesis of Central Florida service center is a state-of-the-art facility staffed by factory-trained and certified Genesis technicians. It provides routine maintenance and complex repairs using genuine Genesis OEM parts and manufacturer specifications.

## Services

- **Routine Maintenance** - oil changes, tire rotations, brake inspections.
- **Advanced Diagnostics** - check-engine light and fault diagnosis.
- **Brake Service** - inspections, repairs, and replacements.
- **Transmission Service** - expert care for transmissions.
- **Tire Service** - alignment and replacements.
- **Battery Service** - battery testing, replacement, and charging system checks.
- Additional repairs as needed across all Genesis models.

## Guest Conveniences

- Complimentary car wash with every service.
- Complimentary Genesis Service Valet - pickup and delivery of the vehicle, plus loaner vehicles.
- Comfortable waiting area with free Wi-Fi, free snacks, free beverages, and gourmet coffee, with TVs and a Bang & Olufsen sound system.
- Online appointment scheduling at https://genesiscfl.com/schedule-service.
