# Genesis of Central Florida

An Open Knowledge Format (OKF) v0.1 bundle describing Genesis of Central Florida, a Genesis dealership at 4500 Collina Terrace, Clermont, FL 34711 serving Lake County and Central Florida. It covers the dealership profile, new and pre-owned Genesis vehicle sales, service, financing, staff, reviews, the model lineup, and AI-optimized discovery endpoints.
